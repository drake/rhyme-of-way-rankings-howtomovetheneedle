Rhyme of Way Rankings

Every pair from Riley Walz’s map, re-sorted by how much the street names actually rhyme — not how fun they are to browse.

Source: Street intersections collected by Riley Walz — Rhyme of Way (5,482 US pairs, data dated 2026-09-05). Walz already ranks the list for discovery (ordinary words, rhythm, recognizable phrases). This page ranks rhyme strength instead: CMUdict rhyme-tail from the last stressed vowel, extra credit for multi-syllable and mosaic phrases, penalties for template families like Hill/Mill and near-identical names.

Rankings computed by How To Move The Needle using the Carnegie Mellon Pronouncing Dictionary. A high score means the names share more sound from the last stressed vowel to the end, with extra weight when both halves of a two-word name lock together. Hill & Mill is a real rhyme; it ranks low because America has 826 of them.
